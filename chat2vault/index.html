📥 chat2vault

Stop losing your best AI conversations.

chat2vault turns any Claude.ai or ChatGPT conversation into a clean Markdown note and drops it straight into your Obsidian vault — no Obsidian plugin required.

  • One-click save to Obsidian (via obsidian:// URI)
  • YAML frontmatter: title, source URL, platform, date, tags
  • Code blocks, tables, lists and links preserved
  • Preview and edit the Markdown before saving
  • Copy as Markdown or download as .md
  • 100% local — your chats never leave your browser
